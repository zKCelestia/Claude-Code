Claim: After the failed joint committee (CMP) of 26 Nov 2026, the PLFSS 2027 returns to the National Assembly for a new reading in the chamber from 7 Dec 2026. That puts the government's next 49.3 opportunity inside this market's window (28 Nov - 11 Dec). The Prime Minister's stated preference is a compromise that lets him use 49.3, not budget ordinances, although ordinances became legally available on 28 Nov.

Dated facts:
1) Public Sénat, 26 Nov 2026 08:41 UTC (updated 10:47 UTC), https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/politique/budget-2027-de-la-securite-sociale-echec-de-la-tentative-de-compromis-entre-deputes-et-senateurs
- The CMP on the PLFSS 2027 failed on the morning of 26 Nov.
- The 50-day period the Constitution gives Parliament on the PLFSS "expires in a few hours, on 27 November at midnight". After that the executive can use ordinances. Several ministers said they would do everything to avoid that.
- The other route is 49.3. The Socialists are "not ready to favour adoption of the PLFSS, as last year" (senator Annie Le Houérou is quoted).
- Sébastien Lecornu "took note" of the CMP failure that morning and is to receive the parliamentary group presidents next week to look for possible compromises.

2) LCP (National Assembly channel), 26 Nov 2026 05:55 CET, https://lcp.fr/actualites/budget-de-la-secu-deputes-et-senateurs-vont-tenter-de-se-mettre-d-accord-ce-jeudi
- An ordinance "is evidently not the intention" of the PM. Without a majority, Lecornu "wants to give himself more time to try to reach an overall compromise on the budget texts (State and Social Security), which would allow him to use 49.3 without being toppled".
- Lecornu on X (Tuesday 24 Nov): "Je recevrai la semaine prochaine chacun des présidents de groupe parlementaire pour poursuivre les discussions budgétaires."

3) Tripalio, 26 Nov 2026 10:35 UTC, https://presse.tripalio.fr/plfss-2027-apres-le-desaccord-de-la-cmp-le-texte-revient-a-lassemblee/
- After the CMP disagreement, the text returns for a new reading at the National Assembly, first in committee and then in the chamber from 7 December.

What this means for the market: a 49.3 on the PLFSS would have to be invoked in the chamber during the new reading. That reading starts on 7 Dec, so 7-11 Dec is the main opportunity inside the window. The government's stated plan (group-president talks next week, then a 49.3 backed by a non-censure arrangement) points to that week. The two main risks for "Yes" are timing that slips past 11 Dec while the talks continue, and a switch to ordinances, which ministers say they want to avoid.
