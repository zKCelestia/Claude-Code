Fact: On 27 Nov 2026 the Upper House Diet-affairs chiefs of the LDP (Isozaki Yoshihiko) and the CDP (Saito Yoshitaka) broadly agreed to resume the House of Councillors special committee on the consumption-tax cut bills on 2 December 2026.

Source: Kyodo News, 27 Nov 2026 19:53 JST (10:53 UTC), carried by Livedoor News: https://news.livedoor.com/article/detail/32920166/ (read 27 Nov 2026 15:20 UTC). Original text: "自民党の磯崎仁彦参院国対委員長と立憲民主党の斎藤嘉隆国対委員長は27日、消費税減税関連法案を審議する参院特別委員会について12月2日に再開することで大筋合意した。" Translation: the two parties' Upper House Diet-affairs chiefs broadly agreed on the 27th to resume the special committee deliberating the consumption-tax cut bills on 2 December.

Why it matters for this market: the market resolves Yes only on an Upper House plenary approval by 9 Dec 2026 14:59 UTC (23:59 JST); committee passage alone does not count. The committee was stalled over the censure fight, which ended with the rejection of the censure motion on the morning of 27 Nov. The ruling parties had said they aimed to resume early. This agreement sets the restart for 2 Dec, five days after the censure vote. It also shows that the largest opposition party has agreed to return to deliberations, so the opposition is no longer refusing to deliberate.

Working: from Wed 2 Dec to Wed 9 Dec 2026 there are 6 weekdays (2, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9 Dec). In that window the bill must finish committee questioning, pass a committee vote where the ruling bloc has 19 of 39 members (Nikkei, 30 Oct 2026), and pass a plenary vote. The government's own target is enactment within the session ending 12 Dec, which is three days after this market's deadline.
