Claim: On 27 Nov 2026 (about 11:30 JST) the House of Councillors plenary REJECTED the opposition censure motion against Education Minister Seki Yoshihiro. The ruling bloc mustered a floor majority in a chamber where it holds only 119 of the 123 seats needed. The government says it will now restart the stalled food consumption-tax cut bill quickly and aims to enact it by 12 Dec. This censure motion against Education Minister Seki Yoshihiro was co-sponsored by Komeito and the Democratic Party for the People (DPFP). Their votes are what the ruling coalition needs to pass the food consumption-tax cut bill in the House of Councillors, where it has no majority.

Dated facts:
1) Mainichi, 27 Nov 2026 11:33 JST (https://mainichi.jp/articles/20261127/k00/00m/010/074000c):
- The Upper House rejected the censure motion against Seki at the morning plenary, by a majority of LDP, Ishin and others voting against.
- PM Takaichi will keep Seki in office.
- LDP and Ishin together hold 119 seats, short of the 123 needed for a majority, so they had been courting Nippon Hoshuto and independents.
- Questioning on the consumption-tax cut bills now in the Upper House had stalled over Seki's party issue. After the rejection, the government and ruling parties aim to resume deliberations early and enact the bill within the session ending 12 Dec. The opposition has lost its "censure card".
Nikkei, 27 Nov 2026 11:33 JST (https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUA270RH0X21C26A1000000/): "The Upper House rejected the censure resolution against Education Minister Seki by majority at today's plenary." Bloomberg's headline gives the margin as 4 votes.
2) Nikkei, 26 Nov 2026 10:19 JST (https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUA248HS0U6A121C2000000/): eight opposition groups submitted the censure motion against Education Minister Seki on 26 Nov, over his having bureaucrats explain policy to related companies at his political fundraising party, and demanded his resignation. Co-sponsors: CDP, DPFP, Komeito, Sanseito, JCP, Inochi, Okinawa no Kaze, SDP. A vote at the 27 Nov plenary was expected to be close.
3) Jiji, 8 Nov 2026 (https://www.jiji.com/jc/article?k=2026110700339&g=pol): the bill passed the House of Representatives on 6 Nov. The ruling LDP and Ishin are a minority in the Upper House, so opposition cooperation is indispensable. The ruling side is targeting the DPFP and Komeito, but both voted against the bill in the lower house. Upper House deliberations began on 11 Nov, and the special committee on the consumption-tax/support-payment bills has been taking questions since 13 Nov (NHK, 12 Nov 2026: https://news.web.nhk/newsweb/na/nd-20261112de74498). Central and local public hearings of the special committee were held on 24 Nov (House of Councillors site, https://www.sangiin.go.jp/). The session runs until 12 Dec (same site).
4) Committee arithmetic (Nikkei, 30 Oct 2026, https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUA281S80Y6A021C2000000/): the LDP and CDP agreed to set up the Upper House special committee for the food-consumption-tax 1% bill. The chair is from the LDP, but there are 19 ruling-bloc members against 20 opposition members, so a united opposition can reject the bill in committee. The opposition also secured more deliberation time than in the lower house, plus central and local public hearings.
