# Historical Case Study: GitHub Core Clone

**Snapshot:** 15 November 2026, approximately 12:20 WIB.  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate the difference between paid cutoff and finalist qualification cutoff. Figures are historical.

## Verified state in the report

- Score: **942**.
- Rank: **#42 of 2,415 ranked entries**.
- Paid positions: top 20.
- Rank-20 score at the snapshot: **945**.
- Final qualification cut: top 60, with rank-60 score **941**.
- Therefore the entry was one point above the qualification bar but three points below the paid bar.

## Decision lesson

The project had to defend a valuable qualification position even though it was outside the paid zone. A risky resubmission could replace a good score with a worse fresh draw. Profiling and repeated tests showed no convincing gain from micro-optimizations, so the correct decision was not to resubmit a lateral variant merely to gamble.

## General lesson

Always separate:
- A score that qualifies an entry for a final stage.
- A score that currently sits in a paid position.
- The final result after the competition's later judging stage.

A good agent protects valuable progress and does not chase every leaderboard movement with an unmeasured resubmission.
